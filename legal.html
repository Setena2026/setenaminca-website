Staying at Se'Tena

Se'Tena Tierra Húmeda has been designed as a place for rest, connection with nature and meaningful experiences. Our surroundings invite guests to slow down, reconnect and enjoy the forest while respecting both the environment and fellow travelers.

During your stay, we kindly ask you to observe the following guidelines:

  • Access is limited to registered guests only.
  • Smoking is not permitted inside cabins or indoor areas.
  • Pets are not allowed.
  • Please help us maintain a peaceful atmosphere by avoiding excessive noise after 9:00 p.m.
  • We encourage the responsible use of water, electricity and all shared spaces.

Check-in & Guest Registration

Each guest must present a valid physical identification document upon arrival.

  • Colombian Citizens: National ID Card or Foreigner Identification Card.
  • International Guests: Valid Passport.

Guests who are unable to provide the required documentation will not be permitted to check in.

Reservation transfers are permitted only upon prior written request sent to se.tenaminca@gmail.com. All requests are subject to approval by Se'Tena Tierra Húmeda and, once approved, will incur an administrative fee of COP 200,000.

Property Conditions

Se'Tena Tierra Húmeda is located in the heart of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, surrounded by tropical rainforest.

Due to the natural topography of the property and its architectural concept, movement throughout the hotel is primarily via staircases, natural pathways and uneven terrain.

For this reason, Se'Tena currently does not provide suitable accessibility for guests with reduced mobility or for those requiring special mobility assistance within the property.

Before making a reservation, we kindly encourage guests to consider these conditions to ensure that Se'Tena is the right destination for their needs and expectations.

Should you have any questions regarding accessibility or the property's layout, our team will be happy to provide additional information before your arrival.
